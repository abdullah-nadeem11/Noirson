    What's inside

    • Streams without an account

      Search songs, albums, artists and playlists from YouTube Music. No sign-in needed.

    • Plays your own files

      Music already on your phone sits in the same library as everything you stream.

    • Synced lyrics

      Lines follow the song as it plays. Tap any line to jump straight to it, just like the sample above.

    • Song radio and autoplay

      Start any song and Noirson keeps going with YouTube's radio for it. When the queue runs out, similar songs keep coming.

    • A queue you control

      Jump to a track, reorder, remove, play next, or add to the end. Shuffle, repeat and seek work as expected.

    • Your library, on your phone

      Favourites, your own playlists, recently played and recent searches are all saved locally.

    • Keeps playing in the background

      Notification and lock-screen controls let you skip and pause without opening the app.

    Noirson is an original Kotlin and Jetpack Compose app, inspired by the desktop players Sonora and LiMusic. This is a debug build, so Android may warn that it comes from an unknown source.

    Streaming uses NewPipeExtractor and is outside YouTube's terms of service, so use it for personal listening only. If search or playback stops working, update the app.
